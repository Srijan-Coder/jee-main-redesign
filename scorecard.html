Multi-Shift Normalization Formula

PROCEDURE

To ensure fairness across different shifts with varying difficulty levels, NTA uses the globally standard Percentile calculation:

Percentile Score (P) =
100 × (No. of candidates in the session with raw score ≤ Candidate's score)
Total number of candidates who appeared in that session

Percentile scores are calculated up to 7 decimal places (e.g. 99.8419204) to avoid ties and eliminate bunching effects across 1.41 million candidates.

Reference: National Normalization Framework Guidelines
