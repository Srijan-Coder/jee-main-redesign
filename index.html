Comprehensive pattern for B.E./B.Tech, B.Arch, and B.Planning

  • Total Marks300 Marks
  • Total Questions90 (Attempt 75)
  • Duration3 Hours (180 mins)
  • Marking+4 Correct, -1 Incorrect
